OUR STORY

We’re building payroll, attendance and HR that African businesses can trust.

RecruitConnect started as a recruitment agency in Lagos. We saw the same pain everywhere: spreadsheets for people, WhatsApp for leave, Excel for payroll, and no single truth for “what is happening right now?”. OneHR is our answer — one place where payroll, attendance, people records and reporting finally agree with each other, secure and audit-ready by design.
